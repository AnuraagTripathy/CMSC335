Chess Openings

The opening is the first part of a chess game. Both players develop their pieces, fight for the center and try to reach a position they understand. You do not need to memorize a lot of moves to play a good opening.

Common Openings

Openings you will see a lot
Opening First Moves Played By
Italian Game 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4 White
Ruy Lopez 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5 White
Sicilian Defense 1.e4 c5 Black
French Defense 1.e4 e6 Black
Queen's Gambit 1.d4 d5 2.c4 White
London System 1.d4 d5 2.Bf4 White

Rules For The Opening

  1. Control the center.
  2. Develop your knights and bishops early.
  3. Castle and connect your rooks.
