Chess Openings

A short guide to the first few moves of a chess game.

Why Openings Matter

The opening is the part of the game where both players develop their pieces, fight for the center and decide what kind of middlegame they want. Most of the time you are not trying to win material in the first ten moves, you are trying to get a position you understand better than your opponent does.

Openings are usually grouped into open games, closed games, semi-open games and flank openings, depending on the first moves played by White and Black.

Common Openings

Openings You Will See Over And Over
Opening First Moves Played By Style
Italian Game 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4 White Classical
Ruy Lopez 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5 White Positional
Sicilian Defense 1.e4 c5 Black Sharp
French Defense 1.e4 e6 Black Solid
Caro-Kann 1.e4 c6 Black Solid
Queen's Gambit 1.d4 d5 2.c4 White Positional
King's Indian Defense 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 g6 Black Aggressive
London System 1.d4 d5 2.Bf4 White Quiet
Move order can change, the ideas behind each opening do not.

Three Rules For The Opening

  1. Control the center with your pawns and pieces.
  2. Develop your knights and bishops before moving the same piece twice.
  3. Castle early and connect your rooks.
